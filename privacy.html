Privacy

Last updated: October 2026.

This website

This site has no analytics, no advertising trackers and no cookies. Your language choice is remembered in your browser only.

The application

StageForge processes your audio on your computer only; no audio ever leaves it. To activate and renew your licence the application sends to our license server: the license key, an anonymous computer identifier (a one-way hash), the computer name, the Windows user name and the e-mail you enter. We use this only to manage your licence (for example, to show you which computers use it and to let you free a seat). It is not sold or shared.

Payments

Payments are handled by Lemon Squeezy, our merchant of record. We never see your card details. Lemon Squeezy shares with us your name, e-mail, country and the status of your subscription so we can provide your licence. See the Lemon Squeezy privacy policy for how they process your data.

Your rights

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the data linked to your licence at any time through the contact address on the main page.
