Built for real-world interfaces

Noise gone. Guitar intact.

Cheap USB interfaces hum, hiss and buzz. StageForge learns the noise floor of your setup from the pauses between your phrases and keys its gates from the clean DI of your guitar, so pick attacks stay instant.

Three dedicated tools do the rest: Hum Killer removes 50 or 60 Hz mains hum and its harmonics, the Hiss Reducer takes out hiss transparently and the Smart Gate keeps the pauses silent.

Every preset is tested against a real noise recording: silence between phrases, full sustain while you play, and pick attacks that come through instantly.

  • 90+ dBless noise in the pauses (high-gain test chain)
  • < 2 dBlost on the first millisecond of a pick attack
  • 0 dBlost on soft passages after long strumming

Questions

What do I need to run StageForge?

A 64-bit Windows 10 or 11 PC and any guitar audio interface (ASIO or Windows Audio). The VST3 plug-in works in any DAW that supports VST3. An Android version is coming soon to Google Play; a macOS version is not available yet.

How does the free trial work?

Download StageForge for Windows, open it and press Start free trial. Everything is unlocked for 14 days and no payment details are needed. On Android, Google Play gives new subscribers 7 days of StageForge Pro for free.

Do I need to be online?

Only to activate and to renew the license from time to time. StageForge keeps working offline for up to 14 days between checks.

What happens if I cancel?

StageForge keeps working until the end of the period you already paid. After that it runs in demo mode. Your presets and recordings are always yours.

Can I use it on two computers?

Yes, on two at the same time. To move it to another computer, open the License screen and press Deactivate this computer.

Who processes the payments?

Lemon Squeezy is our online reseller (merchant of record). They handle the payment, the taxes of your country and your receipts, and let you manage or cancel the subscription.

Will it work with my cheap USB interface?

That is exactly what StageForge was tuned on. The built-in Setup Doctor checks your level, input channel, hum and latency and fixes what it can.

Is there a version for phones and tablets?

Yes. StageForge for Android is coming soon to Google Play. It runs in landscape on phones and tablets, with low-latency audio and support for USB audio interfaces. It is free to download, and StageForge Pro (US$6.99/month, 7-day free trial for new subscribers) unlocks everything.

What is free in the Android version?

The Clean '65 and Brit 68 amps, the Cabinet, Emerald Drive, Studio Reverb and Smart Gate, automatic noise control, the tuner, metronome, Setup Doctor, practice stats, backing-track playback, the five Starter presets and tone codes. You can also hear any Pro block for 30 seconds before deciding.

Does one subscription cover Windows and Android?

No, they are separate purchases. The Windows subscription is sold through Lemon Squeezy and StageForge Pro for Android through Google Play, and each one is managed and cancelled in its own store.
