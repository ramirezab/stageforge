Built for real-world interfaces

Noise gone. Guitar intact.

Cheap USB interfaces hum, hiss and buzz. StageForge learns the noise of your setup from the pauses between your phrases, keys its gates from the clean signal of the guitar and removes mains hum at 50 or 60 Hz automatically.

Every preset is tested against a real noise recording: silence between phrases, full sustain while you play, and pick attacks that come through instantly.

  • 90+ dBless noise in the pauses (high-gain test chain)
  • < 2 dBlost on the first millisecond of a pick attack
  • 0 dBlost on soft passages after long strumming

Questions

What do I need to run StageForge?

A 64-bit Windows 10 or 11 PC and any guitar audio interface (ASIO or Windows Audio). The VST3 plug-in works in any DAW that supports VST3. A macOS version is not available yet.

How does the free trial work?

Download StageForge, open it and press Start free trial. Everything is unlocked for 14 days and no payment details are needed.

Do I need to be online?

Only to activate and to renew the license from time to time. StageForge keeps working offline for up to 14 days between checks.

What happens if I cancel?

StageForge keeps working until the end of the period you already paid. After that it runs in demo mode. Your presets and recordings are always yours.

Can I use it on two computers?

Yes, on two at the same time. To move it to another computer, open the License screen and press Deactivate this computer.

Who processes the payments?

Lemon Squeezy is our online reseller (merchant of record). They handle the payment, the taxes of your country and your receipts, and let you manage or cancel the subscription.

Will it work with my cheap USB interface?

That is exactly what StageForge was tuned on. The built-in Setup Doctor checks your level, input channel, hum and latency and fixes what it can.
